Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Ares Web Services collects, why, and who helps us process it. The short version: your business data is yours, we use it only to run the apps for you, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and sign-in method (email or Google).
  • Workspace data: what you and your team enter, such as staff names, availability, shifts, work orders, customers, notes and files.
  • Billing details: your plan and payment status. Card details go straight to Stripe; we never receive them.
  • Messages you send us, for example through the contact form or by email.

How we use it

To run the apps, sync your data across your team's devices, process payments, answer support questions, prevent abuse, and improve the apps. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who helps us

  • Google Firebase stores your workspace data and handles sign-in.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Anthropic powers the AI assistants. When you use one, your question and a snapshot of the relevant schedule or work orders are sent to Anthropic to produce the answer. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this data isn't used to train its models.
  • FormSubmit delivers messages sent through the contact form on this website to our inbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website, and Google Fonts serves its typefaces (your browser requests them directly from Google).

Each processes information only as needed to provide its service to us.

What we don't do

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, analytics trackers or tracking pixels on this website or in the apps. The apps keep your sign-in in your browser's storage so you stay signed in. We don't keep transcripts of AI assistant conversations; we only count how many questions each workspace asks, to apply usage limits.

Demo mode

The demo versions of the apps run entirely in your browser tab. Nothing you enter in a demo is sent to us, and it's erased when you close the tab.

Your team's information

If you invite staff into your workspace, you're responsible for having the right to add their information. Staff can see what the workspace owner shares with them, such as published schedules or the jobs assigned to them.

Keeping it safe

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our providers, and access between workspaces is blocked by security rules. No system is perfectly secure, so if we ever learn of a breach that affects your data, we'll tell you promptly.

How long we keep it

We keep your workspace data while your account is open. Email us to delete it, and we'll remove it within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep billing records.

Your choices

You can view, change and download your data in the apps at any time, and ask us to correct or delete anything else we hold about you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA; email us and we'll help.

Children

The apps are for businesses and aren't meant for anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and let account owners know.
