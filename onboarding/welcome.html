ARES.How we work
The process

From first conversation
to a confident launch.

Your Statement of Work sets the exact dates. Here’s the shape of it.

Week 1

Discovery & direction

A kickoff call to refine goals, audience, pages and priorities. I confirm the content plan and what I’ll need from you.

Weeks 2–3

Design

I share a design direction for your key pages. You send one consolidated round of feedback; we refine together.

Weeks 3–5

Build

I develop the site on a private preview link, connecting forms, analytics and any agreed integrations.

Week 6

Review & refinement

We walk through every page and key user journey. Two revision rounds are included at each review stage.

Launch

Approval, payment & go-live

After your written approval and the final payment, I launch the site and hand over access, files and a short walkthrough.

Ongoing

Support & visibility

Any maintenance, SEO or marketing in your Statement of Work begins here, with regular reporting.

ARES.Before we begin
Your part

A little preparation
goes a long way.

01

Your brief

Short answers are fine. We’ll go deeper at kickoff.

  • What your business does and who your ideal customer is
  • What visitors should do: call, book, buy or enquire
  • The pages and features that matter most
  • What works and doesn’t on your current site
  • Two or three sites you admire, and why
02

Your materials

I’ll send a private upload link for files.

  • Logo files, brand colors and fonts
  • Page copy, services and contact details
  • Photos you own or have licensed
  • Testimonials you have permission to use
  • Domain and hosting account names
Keep your accounts secure. Never email passwords. For hosting, domain or analytics access, add me as a user from your account, or share through a password manager. I’ll walk you through it.
