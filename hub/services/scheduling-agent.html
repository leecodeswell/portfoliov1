What it does

Everything you need, and nothing you don’t.

A setup wizard asks a few yes-or-no questions about your business. Each yes turns on a feature, so the app only shows what you’ll use. Switch between Simple and Advanced any time.

One-click schedules

Generate a full week that respects everyone’s availability and hours. Lock any shift, then regenerate the rest.

Staff join by link

Send each person a link. They see their shifts, send availability, and add shifts to their calendar.

Roles and requirements

Require a manager or keyholder on certain shifts. It’s never left uncovered by mistake.

Pairings and preferences

Keep certain people apart, pair people who work well together, and honor the shifts people prefer.

Fair hours and rest

Spread hours evenly within each person’s range, and avoid closing then opening the next morning.

Shift cover

When someone calls out, get a ranked list of who can cover, then ask them in order.

AI helper

Ask in plain words, like “Give Ben Friday off and cover his shift.” Review the change, apply it with one click.

Your look

Your name, logo, and colors throughout, for you and your staff.

Review, then publish

Check notes and hours before anyone sees the schedule, then publish to everyone at once.

Questions

Good to know.

Does my team need to download anything?

No. It runs in the browser on any phone or computer. You send each person an invite link; they open it and sign in.

How does it decide who works when?

It fills every shift while respecting availability and maximum hours first, then balances the things you turn on: roles, pairings, preferences, fair hours, and rest. Anything it can’t satisfy shows up as a note, so nothing is hidden.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Everything you set up stays. Pick a plan to keep making changes. Nothing is charged automatically, because no card is needed to start.

Is my data private?

Your workspace is only visible to you and the people you invite, and each person only sees what their role allows. You can download your data any time.

Can you change it to fit my business?

Yes. I can tailor the app to your rules, terms, and look, or build features you need. Ask for a quote; it's agreed in writing before any work starts.
