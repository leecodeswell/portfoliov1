What it does

Everything you need, and nothing you don’t.

A setup wizard asks a few yes-or-no questions about your business. Each yes turns on a feature, so the app only shows what you’ll use. Switch between Simple and Advanced any time.

Bring your spreadsheet

Import Excel or CSV. Match your columns once, then get a report of exactly what came in and what needs a look.

Numbers that never clash

Each job gets the next number automatically, like WO-2026-0042, even when two people save at once.

Board and list views

Drag jobs across a status board, or search, filter, sort, and export the list.

Shop-floor view

Staff see their jobs on their phones and update status, holds, and notes with a tap.

Roles

Owner, admin, manager, staff, and viewer. Each person only changes what their role allows.

Full history

Every change is recorded: who changed what, and when.

Printable travelers

Print a clean job sheet to send along with the work.

Your statuses

Use your own steps, like Waiting on material or Inspection, with reasons for holds.

Your look

Your shop name, logo, and colors throughout.

Questions

Good to know.

Does my team need to download anything?

No. It runs in the browser on any phone or computer. You send each person an invite link; they open it and sign in.

Will my spreadsheet import cleanly?

The import matches your columns, spots near-duplicate customers and people, and shows a report of every row before anything is saved. Messy dates and statuses are handled for you.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Everything you set up stays. Pick a plan to keep making changes. Nothing is charged automatically, because no card is needed to start.

Is my data private?

Your workspace is only visible to you and the people you invite, and each person only sees what their role allows. You can download your data any time.

Can you change it to fit my business?

Yes. I can tailor the app to your rules, terms, and look, or build features you need. Ask for a quote; it's agreed in writing before any work starts.
