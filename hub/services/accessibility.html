Accessibility

Last updated October 12, 2026

Ares Web Services wants this website and its apps (Scheduling Agent and Work Order Manager) to be usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, zoom or other assistive technology.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA. We build with labeled form fields, keyboard-reachable controls, visible focus outlines, readable color contrast, and layouts that work when zoomed or on a phone, and we test with automated checkers and by hand.

Known limits

Some parts are still being improved. The animated diagram at the top of the home page is decorative; the same information is in the text around it, and it stays still if your device is set to reduce motion. Drag-and-drop on the work order board also has a button-based alternative on each job.

Tell us about a problem

If something on the site or in the apps is hard to use, email thompsontechnologiesleet@gmail.com with the page and what happened. We'll reply within 2 business days and work with you to fix it or give you the information another way.
